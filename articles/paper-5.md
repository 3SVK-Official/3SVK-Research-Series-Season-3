### Abstract
This study investigates automated anomaly detection techniques for enterprise network security. By employing heuristic pattern analysis and continuous log auditing, we evaluate threat response timelines and mitigation efficiencies.

### 1. Introduction
Enterprise perimeters face increasingly sophisticated cyber intrusions. Manual log reviews are often too slow to prevent active breaches. Automated detection frameworks provide real-time visibility and rapid containment capabilities.

### 2. Methodology
The proposed security model utilizes behavioral baselines to flag unexpected traffic spikes and unauthorized access attempts. Automated isolation scripts trigger immediately upon detecting severe protocol anomalies.

### 3. Conclusion
Integrating automated threat detection minimizes dwell time for malicious actors and protects core organizational assets. Future research will focus on adaptive machine-learning models to predict novel attack vectors.
