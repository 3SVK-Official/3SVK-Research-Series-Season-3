# Research Gap

## Current Status

The final research gap has not yet been established.

This project will determine the research gap through a structured
review of existing scientific and operational systems followed by
baseline experiments.

## Why the Gap Requires Investigation

Satellite-based monitoring systems already exist for floods,
forest disturbances and wildfires.

Examples include:

- Copernicus Global Flood Monitoring for flood monitoring using
  Sentinel-1 SAR observations.
- Global Forest Watch integrated disturbance alerts, which combine
  multiple optical and radar-based alert systems.
- NASA FIRMS for active-fire and thermal-anomaly monitoring using
  satellite observations including VIIRS and MODIS.

Therefore, simply creating another satellite-based detection model
would not by itself constitute a sufficient research contribution.

## Investigation Strategy

The team will investigate:

1. What existing systems already detect.
2. What satellite and environmental data they use.
3. What algorithms or processing methods they employ.
4. How their outputs are validated.
5. What limitations have been documented.
6. Under which environmental or observational conditions failures occur.
7. Whether combining complementary observations addresses any of
   those documented limitations.
8. Whether the improvement can be measured experimentally.

## Initial Research Hypothesis

The initial hypothesis to be tested is:

> Complementary satellite and environmental observations may reduce
> certain false alarms and missed detections compared with using a
> single observation source, but the improvement may vary according
> to hazard type, environment and observation conditions.

This is a hypothesis rather than a confirmed result.

## Required Evidence

Before claiming a final research contribution, the team will provide:

- Literature evidence.
- Baseline implementation or reproduction.
- Clearly defined datasets.
- Experimental methodology.
- Evaluation metrics.
- Comparative results.
- Error analysis.
- Limitations.

## Research Integrity

The repository will not claim novelty, accuracy improvement or
superiority before the relevant evidence has been produced and
documented.
